Example Project

How a single automation can save a small team 5+ hours a week.

Here's an example of the type of system I build — from the problem through to the result.

The Problem

New enquiries going unanswered for hours

A busy small business misses the response window on new leads — especially evenings and weekends — and competitors reply first.

The Trigger

New enquiry received via email or web form

The automation watches for incoming enquiry emails, extracts the lead's name, what they're interested in, and their contact details automatically.

The Action

Instant personalised reply + CRM log

A warm, branded response is sent to the lead within 60 seconds. Details are simultaneously logged in a Google Sheet and flagged for morning follow-up.

The Follow-Up

Automated booking invitation sent 2 hours later

If the lead hasn't responded, a second message offers three available call or appointment slots — pulling directly from a shared calendar.
